FILE AE-001
Synopsis
Beneath the lit buildings of a city that runs on magic lies the power keeping an entire country alive. In a few months, one person will be handed the keys to all of it.

Theodore Vesper was supposed to win. A kind, unlikely reformer from a small neighborhood nobody took seriously, he gave Little Vesper something it had never had: a voice. Then, in the middle of a speech at a glittering party, he dies. No wound. No poison. No spell.

You were in the room. Now the police want answers, the powerful are circling your neighborhood, and someone is waiting with an offer you will not be able to refuse. The election is coming whether you are ready or not. And something out there has noticed you.
FILE AE-002
Why Play
A Living City
One enormous metropolis of art deco and brutalism. A city that never sleeps. The Concrete Jungle. Politics, nightlife, love and crime share the same streets.
An Election that could blow up the world
Giant factions, kingmakers, rallies, scandals and backroom deals. Back a candidate, break one, or ignore the whole thing.
Your Story, Not Ours (It kind of is tho)
A nonlinear sandbox. No rails, no required path. The city keeps moving with you or without you.
What will you do with a destiny that is not your?
Every character carries something bigger than themselves: The universe demands you to fulfill your role. A life they would rather (not) have.
Dark, With a Pulse
Real stakes and real stakes (like... for vampires).
Mysteries (not) Worth Solving
Someone is pulling threads behind the scenes. Clues are scattered across the city. You decide whether to follow them.
Any Ending
Salvation, ruin, or something nobody saw coming. The fate of the city is decided by what you do.
FILE AE-003
The World at a Glance
The City
 ■■ districts, one metropolis. Little Vesper is your home: a small, close-knit neighborhood that looks after its own.
Magic
Magic is life. Subways, power, media and security run on it. Elves, orcs, vampires, witches and undead are ordinary neighbors, in a modern D&D-esque world.
Power
The presidency commands the magical infrastructure the country runs on. Whoever wins holds this power, and few voters understand how it works.
The Divine
Gods need believers, and they are as strange as the people who worship them. None are safe to ignore.
